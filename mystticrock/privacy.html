Privacy policy

MystticRock, a game for learning guitar and bass, for Windows.
Last updated 19 November 2026.

In short

MystticRock has no accounts, no telemetry, no ads and no server of its own. What you do in the game stays on your computer. The game goes online only when you check for a new version and when you use YouTube in it: to check a video link in the import and to play a video next to the neck.

Kept on your computer

  • Settings (including the language and the audio devices), calibration, your instruments and their photos, best scores, the tempo and part level of each song, your tones and presets, and the game's logs: in %APPDATA%\MystticRock.
  • Your songs: in Documents\MystticRock\Utwory. The game's own songs, prepared on the first start: in %LOCALAPPDATA%\MystticRock.
  • With the diagnostics overlay (F3) open during a song: also a recording of your guitar and a log of the scoring, in %APPDATA%\MystticRock\logs.
  • The installer of an update you download: in %TEMP%\MystticRock (the next update replaces it).
  • When a YouTube video plays: the player's cookies and similar data, in %APPDATA%\MystticRock\webview.

The game listens to your instrument through the audio input you choose and analyses the sound on your computer; the sound is never sent anywhere. Your files stay when you update or uninstall the game, and nothing in them leaves your computer. To delete them, delete these folders.

New version (GitHub)

When you press Check now in Settings, About, or once a day if you turn on daily checking (off by default), the game asks GitHub for the list of the game's releases. When you choose Download and install, it downloads the installer from GitHub. GitHub receives your IP address and the technical data of each request; the game sends nothing that identifies you or your computer. The GitHub General Privacy Statement applies.

YouTube (Google)

MystticRock uses YouTube API Services.

  • When you check a video link in the song import, the game asks YouTube for the video's title and channel and whether its author allows playing it outside YouTube. The song pack keeps the video's address, title and channel.
  • When you play a song with a YouTube video, the official YouTube player plays it in a small window next to the neck, through Microsoft Edge WebView2, which is part of Windows. YouTube serves the video, and may show its own ads in it, as on any website with an embedded video.
  • YouTube receives your IP address, the video you play and technical data about the player and your computer. The player may store cookies and similar data in %APPDATA%\MystticRock\webview.
  • The game does not sign you in to YouTube or access your YouTube account, does not download videos and does not take their sound.
  • The Google Privacy Policy applies to what YouTube receives, and the YouTube Terms of Service apply to using YouTube in the game. Before you use YouTube in the game for the first time, the game asks you to accept them and this policy.

Links

Links in the game, such as Game website, open in your web browser, and the site you open has its own policy.

What the game does not do

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • No telemetry, usage statistics or crash reports.
  • No ads of its own.
  • The author of the game receives no data from it, so there is nothing to sell or share.

Changes

If the game starts handling data differently, this page changes before the version that does it is released, and the date at the top changes with it.

Contact
